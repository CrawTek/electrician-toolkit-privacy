Red Horizon Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Developer: CrawTek
App: Red Horizon
Android package: com.crawtek.redhorizon

Red Horizon displays watch information locally. CrawTek does not collect or share personal data through this watch face.

Information used on your watch

Red Horizon is a Wear OS watch face. The Wear OS watch-face runtime uses the current time and date, your device's time-format setting, battery percentage, daily step count, and step-goal progress to render the display. Step information is used only to show your daily activity on the watch. Red Horizon does not create a separate activity history, send these values to CrawTek, or upload them to a server.

The fictional planet background is an included decorative animation. It does not use your location, live satellite imagery, or an internet connection.

Permissions and platform services

The watch-face package contains display resources and a Watch Face Format definition. It requests no Android permissions and includes no executable application code, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK. Wear OS supplies the display data through its system watch-face runtime. Device settings and platform permissions can affect the data available to the face.

Wear OS, Google Play, and any health services you use are separate products. Their own privacy policies govern their independent handling of data. This policy describes Red Horizon and CrawTek's practices; it does not replace those platform policies.

Purchases

Red Horizon is a paid download. Google Play processes purchases and payment information under its own policies. The watch face does not receive your payment-card details. Google may provide CrawTek with sales and transaction records through developer tools; these are used for purchase support, refunds, accounting and applicable legal obligations.

Sharing and tracking

Red Horizon does not transmit user data to CrawTek or other recipients, sell user data, track users, or serve advertising. It has no account, login, cloud synchronization, or in-app purchases.

Retention and deletion

CrawTek holds no user data collected by the watch face. You can remove Red Horizon through your watch's app settings or Google Play. Removing the face does not delete records maintained separately by your watch or health services; manage those records through the corresponding service. CrawTek cannot remotely access your watch or its local data.

Children's privacy

Red Horizon does not collect personal information from users of any age, including children.

If you contact us

If you email CrawTek, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond to your request. Email is handled by the email providers involved. We retain support correspondence only as needed to address the request and applicable obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it is separate from using the watch face. GitHub may process technical information about website visits under its privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the watch face or its privacy practices change. Updated policies will appear here with a revised effective date.
