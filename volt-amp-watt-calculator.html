Volt / Amp / Watt Calculator Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Developer: CrawTek
App: Volt / Amp / Watt Calculator
Android package: appinventor.ai_Steve19Ohio.Calculator

Volt / Amp / Watt Calculator collects no user data and sends no user data off your device.

Information collection and use

The values you enter are processed locally on your device to perform calculations and display results. CrawTek does not collect these inputs or results. The app does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, usage information, or other user data, and does not transmit user data to CrawTek or to third parties.

Sharing, tracking, and third parties

The app does not share or sell user data. It does not transmit data for advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash reporting. This no-collection and no-transmission policy applies to the app and any components included in it.

Retention, deletion, and security

Because no user data is collected or transmitted by the app, CrawTek holds no app-collected user data to retain or delete. You can clear any local app storage or uninstall the app through Android settings. CrawTek cannot remotely access your device or its local app storage.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from users of any age, including children.

If you contact us

If you choose to email CrawTek, your email address and the information you include are handled separately from the app to respond to your request. Email is processed by the email providers involved. We retain support correspondence only as needed to address the request and applicable obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

This policy website

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting this website is separate from using the app. GitHub may process technical information about website visits under its privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or its privacy practices. Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
