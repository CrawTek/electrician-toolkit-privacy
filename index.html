Electrician Toolkit Privacy Policy

Updated October 13, 2026 · Covers app version 0.1.8

Electrician Toolkit is provided by CrawTek.

Information you choose to save

Electrician Toolkit stores the job details you enter, including customer names, site addresses, equipment, notes, inspection results, photos, drawings, calculations, estimates, expenses and payment records. This information is used to provide the app’s job-management and calculation features on your device.

Local processing

The app does not send your records to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs or remote servers, and does not request Internet permission. It does not collect your device location, contacts or advertising identifier.

Photos and files

The Android file picker lets you choose individual images and backup files. The app does not request broad access to your photo library or storage. Imported images are resized and copied into local job records. Importing or deleting a job does not change the original image file.

Exports and printing

When you export a backup or print a report, you choose the file destination or print service through Android. The resulting files may contain customer information and photos. A cloud file provider or print service you select may handle that information under its own policy. The app does not automatically share records, and the developer does not sell them. Exported backup files are not encrypted by this app.

Retention and deletion

Records remain in the app’s private storage until you remove them, clear the app’s data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Delete an individual job from its Overview screen. Automatic app cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. Files you export and original photos remain outside the app and must be deleted separately wherever you saved them. The developer cannot recover or remotely erase your local records.

Security

The app uses Android’s private app storage and atomic file writes. It does not provide separate record encryption or an app password. Protect your device with a screen lock and protect any exported files. No storage method can guarantee against every form of loss or unauthorized access.

Audience and changes

This is a professional electrical field tool, not a service directed to children. Updated versions may revise this policy to describe their actual data practices.

Publisher and privacy contact

CrawTek
For privacy questions or app support, email Steve@CrawTek.com.

If you email CrawTek, the information you choose to include is used to respond to your request. Support correspondence is handled outside the app by email providers and may be retained while needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. Do not send customer records or job backups unless needed for your request. You can ask about deletion of support correspondence at the same address.
